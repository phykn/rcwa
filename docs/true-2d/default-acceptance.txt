At 806 nm, H5->H7 changed T_p by 0.00599218361551, exceeding 5e-4. It was rejected rather than weakening the gate.

Limits
These absolute tolerances certify only the recorded synthetic geometry, angles, material constants, basis and sampled wavelengths. They are not metrology tolerances, relative-error claims for tiny cross-polarized powers, or accuracy guarantees for edited inputs. Unchecked edits remain convergence not assessed. A finite wavelength scan cannot prove absence of all sub-grid resonances.

Keep this historical archive unchanged.
